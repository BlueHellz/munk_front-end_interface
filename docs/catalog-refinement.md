# Catalog and Discovery refinement

The existing frontend now contains 20 fictional Houses, 47 works (19 sample films / 28 essays), two series and two Board threads. Sixteen new identities are Kiln & Clay (Ada Mercer), Night Observatory (Ivo Vale), Glasshouse (Lena Moss), Thread & Tide (Amara Finch), Blue Note Room (Jonah Bell), Paper Orbit (Cleo Hart), Supper Club (Ravi Sen), Tiny Machines (Mira Cole), Wild Atlas (Sol Rivera), Brick by Brick (Owen Lake), Salt & Sail (Esme North), Small Stage (Felix Rowan), Afterimage (Zara Quinn), Moss & Myth (Orla Wren), Slow Mile (Tomas Reed), and Letterpress (Sana West). Every new House has an introduction, creator monogram, owned works, independent configuration and reloadable route. They are not duplicated versions of the four older Houses.

Exchange is a compact six/four/three/two-column canvas with periodic wide/tall tiles, no special oversized first tile. Mobile keeps search/modes visible and advanced controls in Filters & sort. Comparison-only columns are not rendered in mosaic. Price/change remain sample values; details hold charts, volume, capitalization, score and ownership disclosures. No financial service exists.

Navigation now uses the same rail/top bar across Discovery, Exchange, House, video, Board, article and thread. House content retains its own theme and mandatory reserved text-introduction slot.

Discovery configuration adds optional validated discoverySections independently of Classic sections. Missing field means all seven default sections; no old designs/versions are migrated or discarded. The designer supports checkboxes and up/down ordering, draft undo, save and existing version restoration. Search/categories stay visible even when all sections are hidden.

Artwork is generated by scripts/create-house-art.mjs and scripts/create-catalog-art.mjs: 20 distinct House illustrations, 47 unique work illustrations, and 16 new monograms. Vectors are original CC0 and sharp at any viewport. Shared within-House motifs are intentional. Board photos, Maya's original portrait, viewer avatars and some Classic decoration still use temporary screenshot crops; no actual creator footage was supplied. All videos still play the same disclosed eight-second local clip.
